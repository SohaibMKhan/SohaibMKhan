## 🚀 &nbsp;Selected Projects & Solutions

### 🧠 Pixxy — Local AI Desktop Companion

A **desktop-first local AI virtual companion** designed around privacy, personality and offline-first interaction. The project explores a practical architecture where a bundled/local LLM runtime can provide intelligence without making external APIs mandatory.

**Direction:** Desktop AI · Local LLMs · Ollama · AI Agents · Product Engineering

---

### 📊 Enterprise AI-Powered Business Intelligence

Designing an analytics architecture where **Power BI becomes more than a reporting layer** by connecting business data with AI-generated analysis and automated management communication.

**Flow:** Data Sources → Power BI → AI Analysis → Actionable Insights → Automated Communication

**Focus:** KPI intelligence · AI insights · n8n workflows · Management reporting

---

### 🦺 Autonomous Safety Inspection & Intelligence System

An enterprise-oriented concept for transforming safety inspection data into a more intelligent operational system — combining structured inspection data, analytics, recurring-problem detection and AI-assisted insights within a Power BI-centered experience.

**Focus:** Safety analytics · Power BI · AI insights · Recurring issue detection · Decision support

---

### 📈 Sales Visit & Customer Intelligence Platform

A business intelligence and operational workflow concept for managing pipeline customers, sales visits, volumes, organizational roles and alert-based follow-ups.

**Focus:** Customer intelligence · Sales analytics · KPI tracking · Workflow automation · Role-based operations

---

### 📁 OneDrive Document Access & Workflow Monitoring

A native Windows-based monitoring approach for large synced document environments, designed to track file activity and maintain structured logs without modifying the monitored documents.

**Focus:** PowerShell · Windows automation · File-system monitoring · Excel logging · Operational visibility
